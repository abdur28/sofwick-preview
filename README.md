# Sofwick

**Your TV, from your phone.**

Sofwick turns an Android TV or Google TV into something the whole family can use from their phones. No app to
install on the phone: open the TV's page in the browser, enter a four-digit PIN once, and you're in.

> Sofwick is in development. This page shows what it does; the code is private.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Remote** | A real remote on the phone: arrows, OK, Back, Home, play and volume, a touchpad with a cursor, typing into the TV's search boxes, and every app one tap away. |
| **Send to TV** | Photos, videos, PDFs and links from the phone open on the TV at once. |
| **Message Board** | Sticky notes for the family, with photos, pins and reminders that pop up over whatever is on. |
| **Draw** | Draw on the TV together, from several phones at once. |
| **Focus** | A calm timer for work and breaks, with rain, waves or a fireplace. |
| **Game Night** | A quiz for two to six, with everyone's phone as their buzzer and the scores on the TV. |
| **Ask AI** | Ask a question or tell the TV what to do, in plain words. |
| **Ambient wall** | Art, the weather, the picture of the day and your week, when the TV sits idle. |
| **Welcome home** | A greeting on the TV when someone comes home. |
| **Sofwick Home** | An optional, clean Home screen: your apps, live cards for the features, nothing else. |

## How it works

- Everything stays on your home Wi-Fi. The phone talks to the TV directly; nothing goes through a server.
- Works with a VPN on the phone.
- Sofwick Home is optional: the features work with your TV's own Home screen too.

## Requirements

Android TV or Google TV, Android 8 or newer. Tested so far on the Xiaomi TV Box S (2nd Gen) with Google TV 14.
